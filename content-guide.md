## 12. FAQs

Header: "In case you're wondering..." / "faqs"

- **What time should I arrive?** — Seated at `{church}` by `{time}`; ceremony begins promptly at `{time}`. "We'd appreciate it if you could join us at the ceremony, not just at the reception. It's the most important part of our wedding, and sharing that moment with you would mean so much."
- **Where are the ceremony and the reception taking place?** — Ceremony at `{church}`, followed by reception at `{venue}`.
- **What happens after the ceremony?** — "Feel free to head straight to the reception venue and enjoy a delightful cocktail hour at {venue}."
- **Is there a dress code?** — Formal or semi-formal; summary of guest attire.
- **Can we take photos and post them on social media?** — "Yes! We would love for you to take photos and share them. However, please refrain from taking photos during the ceremony and do not block our official photographers."
- **Can I bring a plus one?** — "Due to limited space, we are only able to accommodate those guests formally invited on our wedding invitation. We want to be surrounded by our families, friends, and familiar faces. Please refer to the number of seat/s allocated for you. Thank you for understanding."
- **Can I bring kids?** — "We adore your children; however, we have decided to keep our wedding celebration as much as possible for adults-only. We truly appreciate your understanding and look forward to celebrating together."
- **Until what time is the program?** — "The reception program will wrap up around {time}, but the celebration doesn't have to end there! Join us for an after-party filled with drinks, music, and dancing."
- **How can I contribute to the celebration?** — "Your presence is truly the greatest gift... we would appreciate you following our special notes and attire guide, and if you can, staying with us until the end of the celebration."
- **How can I RSVP?** — RSVP online via form or contact `{groom name + phone}` / `{bride name + phone}`. Deadline `{date}`.
- **For additional questions, who should I contact?** — "please feel free to reach out to us via {Facebook Messenger}."
